# Wave 1 — developer validation kit (manual, owner-executed)

Goal: 10 real conversations with people actively building in this space.
**These messages are sent by the owner from their own accounts — never mass-posted,
never by an agent.** One person, one message, personalized.

## The message (adapt the first line per person)

> I built a small context compiler for agents — not a memory store, the layer
> between storage/retrieval and the model: it ranks, verifies, resolves conflicts
> and budget-fits what enters the context window, and shows a receipt for every
> rejection.
>
> Not asking for a star — I want you to break the selection logic.
> 30-second demo: `npx github:MajidAsghariTabrizi/bandpass demo`
> Interactive: https://majidasgharitabrizi.github.io/bandpass/
>
> Does this abstraction make sense to you? What breaks?

## Who to reach (criteria + where they are)

Find 10 from these pools — people who recently shipped, not celebrities:

1. Authors of small/new MCP servers in punkpeye/awesome-mcp-servers (the ones
   merged in the last month — they read PRs there and will see ours).
2. Contributors to Memory & Memory-adjacent projects: Graphiti, Hindsight,
   OpenViking issue/PR authors (not the core teams first).
3. People filing issues on Mem0/Letta about "context too large", "stale memory",
   "conflicting facts" — they have the exact pain.
4. Authors of the adjacent context tools we benchmarked against in search
   (aegis, one-context, codectx) — peers, potential collaborators.
5. Builders posting context-engineering writeups (the authors of the articles
   in the yzfly/Meirtz lists).
6. Maintainers of agent harnesses looking for a context layer (awesome-harness-engineering list).
